These terms cover the Nasti macOS app and the project website maintained by Tony (@xiaotwu). Nasti provides a local music library, access to a supported catalog of licensed Internet Archive recordings, and optional YouTube Music playlist management. The Nasti Privacy Policy explains how these features handle your information.

YouTube Music and Google authorization

By using Nasti’s YouTube Music integration, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Before enabling the integration in Nasti, review and agree to the Nasti Privacy Policy and these terms. Google handles sign-in and authorization separately in your browser. Connect an account whose playlists you are authorized to manage. You can withdraw authorization in Nasti or in Google Account security settings.

Importing a playlist in Nasti 0.1.2 creates a snapshot for the current app session. Nasti sends the playlist edits you request to YouTube. Check the selected playlist, connected account, and visibility before applying changes. Remove local snapshot removes a copy from Nasti. Delete online playlist sends a deletion request to YouTube. Playback of YouTube Music tracks opens in your system browser and is provided by YouTube.

Internet Archive and the Vault

Nasti’s licensed catalog looks up supported licensed recordings on Internet Archive. Follow the license and attribution information shown by the source. Saving an eligible recording to the Vault creates a local copy for offline listening. Any further use or redistribution depends on the recording’s license. Internet Archive services are subject to Internet Archive’s terms.

Availability and support

Features depend on the installed release, supported macOS version, and the availability and permissions of external services. Nasti may adjust an integration when its provider changes an API, permission, or availability. You can find current releases and contact Tony through the project issue tracker for support. Keep private account or playlist details out of public issues.
