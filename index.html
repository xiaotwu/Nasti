Nasti

Nasti is a music player for Apple Silicon Macs. Browse and play recordings from the supported licensed Internet Archive catalog, organize your library and keep selected tracks in the offline Vault.

The optional YouTube Music integration uses YouTube Data API Services with your Google authorization. Availability depends on the release and Google’s approval status. It imports and manages playlists you choose. YouTube Music playback opens in your browser.

Nasti starts with an empty library. Open Search to find licensed Internet Archive recordings or use Import Playlist to add a supported release.
