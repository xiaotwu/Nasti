This policy explains how Nasti handles information when you use the macOS app or visit its GitHub Pages website. Nasti is maintained by Tony (@xiaotwu). You can use the local library, licensed Internet Archive catalog, and Vault without connecting a Google account.

Google and YouTube data

Nasti uses YouTube API Services for its optional YouTube Music playlist panel. Before connecting, you can review this policy and the Nasti Terms of Use. Google then opens in your system browser and handles account selection, sign-in, and consent. After you grant permission, Google returns an authorization code to Nasti on your Mac. Nasti exchanges that code for OAuth tokens. Nasti does not receive your Google password or browser session cookies, and it does not request your Google email address or profile.

Nasti receives the following data from Google and YouTube.

  • Authorization data. The authorization code and OAuth tokens allow Nasti to make YouTube API requests on your behalf.
  • YouTube channel identifier. Nasti uses it to determine which playlists you can edit and associate your saved playlist copies with the correct account.
  • Playlist information. Nasti uses identifiers, titles, descriptions, visibility, and ownership information to list and refresh playlists. It checks item counts before changing track order and preserves existing settings such as default language and podcast status when updating playlist details.
  • Playlist entries. Nasti uses entry and video identifiers, titles, creator information, and track order to display a playlist, open tracks in YouTube Music, or apply the edits you choose.

Why Nasti requests access

Nasti requests the youtube.force-ssl OAuth scope because the YouTube API requires write permission to edit playlists. Google describes the scope broadly. Nasti uses it to identify your YouTube channel, read and display playlists, and carry out the playlist actions you initiate. These actions include creating a playlist, editing its details or visibility, adding or removing entries, reordering tracks, and deleting a playlist. YouTube applies changes to the account connected in Nasti.

Nasti’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Nasti uses this data for the playlist features described here. Nasti does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling, analytics, or AI training.

Storage and protection

Nasti sends requests to Google and YouTube over HTTPS. The authorization callback stays on your Mac and uses PKCE to protect the code exchange. The app holds short-lived access tokens in memory. On macOS, it uses Keychain to retain the refresh token so it can reconnect when you return to the playlist panel.

If you import a playlist, Nasti stores a snapshot in its SQLite library on your Mac, together with the associated channel identifier and the time it was saved. After 30 days without a refresh, Nasti removes the snapshot when it next opens the library or reads the saved playlist records. If Nasti detects that your Google grant is invalid, it removes your account’s saved snapshots and attempts to remove the unusable token from Keychain.

Nasti stores your local library, Vault copies of eligible recordings, and app preferences on your Mac. The app also keeps a local record of your acceptance of this policy for the YouTube Music feature.

Sharing and service providers

  • Google and YouTube receive OAuth and playlist requests directly from your Mac. When you open a track or playlist in YouTube Music, Nasti gives the relevant identifiers to your system browser to open the matching page. Google and YouTube handle their services and any browser cookies under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app’s releases. The app contacts GitHub when you check for or download an update. GitHub handles requests to these services under its privacy statement.
  • Internet Archive receives search queries, item requests, and download or playback requests when you use its catalog. See the Internet Archive terms and privacy policy.

Nasti has no app server that receives Google tokens or playlist snapshots. It does not send them to GitHub or Internet Archive.

This website stores the theme you select in your browser. Site search runs in your browser and does not send your search terms to Nasti. Nasti does not add advertising or analytics scripts to this website.

Delete records or revoke access

  1. To delete one local playlist record, choose Remove local snapshot in the YouTube Music panel. The playlist remains on YouTube.
  2. To stop Nasti’s access and remove the saved account data, choose Revoke access in the panel’s Google account menu. Nasti asks Google to revoke the grant and removes the account’s token and local snapshots when revocation succeeds.
  3. You can also revoke Nasti’s access in your Google Account security settings. Nasti clears the associated local snapshots when it next detects the invalid grant.

The separate Delete online playlist action removes the selected playlist from YouTube. Removing a local Vault copy frees space on your Mac.

Contact and policy updates

Contact Tony through the Nasti issue tracker with questions, complaints, or requests for help deleting data. GitHub issues are public. Keep account details, private playlist content, passwords, authorization codes, and tokens out of your report.

The effective date above identifies this version of the policy. If Nasti begins using Google user data for a new purpose or accesses additional Google user data, the app will ask for your consent to the updated policy before it makes that change.
