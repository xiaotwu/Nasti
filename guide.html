Install Nasti and build your first library on Apple Silicon macOS.

Install Nasti

  1. Open GitHub Releases and download the Apple Silicon macOS DMG from the latest public release.
  2. Open the disk image, copy Nasti to Applications, then open your installed copy.
  3. Nasti opens to an empty archive. Use the top bar to find recordings, import playlists, or open Settings.

Build your library

Use Search in the top bar to look through your library or the supported licensed Internet Archive catalog. To import a supported release directly, paste its link into Import playlist and review the track list. Choose individual tracks or import the full list in source order.

Each archive column represents a playlist. Use the archive or playlist overview to navigate tracks. Save eligible recordings to the Vault for offline playback.

YouTube Music

Where the integration is enabled for your release and account, open the YouTube Music panel and review the privacy policy and terms of use. Agree to continue, then sign in through your system browser and decide on Google’s consent screen whether to grant Nasti access. Connecting is optional. Playback opens in YouTube Music in your system browser.

Updates and support

Open Settings → About to check for updates where supported, or download a newer version from GitHub Releases. Report a problem on the project issue tracker. Include your app and macOS versions, and leave private playlist or account details out of public reports.
